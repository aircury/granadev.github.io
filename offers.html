Accounting, Tax and Administrative Technician (disability certificate ≥33%)

Job description

We are looking to hire an Accounting, Tax and Administrative Technician with a disability certificate equal to or greater than 33% to manage the company's accounting, tax and administrative functions.

This is an on-site position in Granada, for an organised, proactive person with attention to detail, capable of working autonomously on daily accounting and tax management, as well as supporting the organisation and administrative management of the office.

Main responsibilities

Accounting and tax

  • Maintaining and reviewing the company's accounts based on the documentation provided.
  • Recording issued and received invoices, as well as bank transactions.
  • Bank reconciliations and control of accounting records.
  • Preparing and filing periodic tax returns (VAT, withholdings, instalment payments and other applicable forms).
  • Preparing accounting information for the preparation and filing of annual accounts.
  • Handling and managing tax and accounting notifications, communications and requests.
  • Monitoring and tracking the company's tax obligations.

Administrative and office

  • Issuing, recording and controlling invoices.
  • Document management and physical and digital filing.
  • Preparing reports, databases and spreadsheets.
  • Telephone and email support in Spanish and English.
  • Office space management and basic coordination of its use.
  • Support in organising workshops, activities and events.
  • Managing orders, suppliers and material inventory.
  • Processing administrative documentation and supporting daily company operations.
  • Other administrative tasks necessary for the proper functioning of the organisation.

Requirements

Education

Training in Business Administration and Finance, Accounting, Business Management or similar will be valued.

Experience

  • Previous experience in accounting and/or administration will be valued.
  • Experience in tax filing and basic tax management will be valued.

Languages

  • Native or bilingual Spanish.
  • English level B2 (desirable).

Skills

  • General accounting.
  • Basic taxation (VAT, withholdings and quarterly forms).
  • Proficiency in Excel and Google Sheets.
  • Office software (Microsoft Office / Google Workspace).
  • Administrative, document and organisational management.

Professional profile

We are looking for an organised and methodical person, responsible and capable of working autonomously. It is important to have great attention to detail, as well as a proactive and resourceful attitude towards day-to-day tasks. We also value communication skills and the ability to adapt to varied functions within the company. A practical test will be carried out during the selection process to assess the technical competencies required for the position.

Essential requirements

  • Disability certificate equal to or greater than 33%.
  • Residence in Granada or surrounding areas.
  • Approximate English level B2.
  • Fluent use of office software tools.
  • No criminal record, due to access to sensitive information.

Employment conditions

  • Permanent contract with a 6-month probation period.
  • Full-time on-site schedule.
  • Salary commensurate with experience.
  • Flexible working hours.
  • Annual salary review.
  • Incentive system based on objectives.
  • Professional development opportunities within the company.
